A living sanctuary

Rooted in nature,
raised by craft

Bambu Indah began as a private collection of restored antique Javanese bridal homes and grew into one of Bali’s most singular retreats. Each structure — from sculpted bamboo to century-old teak — is set within organic gardens, spring-fed pools and the green hush of the valley.

Here, luxury is quiet: farm-to-table dining at Dapur, river swims, and mornings that smell of frangipani and rain.
